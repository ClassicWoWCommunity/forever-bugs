# Rules:

### 1. **THIS IS NOT A PLACE TO AIR GRIEVANCES**
* Please use the official forums for broader discussion or impassioned arguments.
* This GitHub is intended for bug reports.
  * If it is unclear whether something is a bug, feel free to submit a report. You may receive a response or additional context.
  * If you disagree with the response, you may present a well-researched and constructive argument in the replies. However, the official forums are generally a better venue, as the discussion will be visible to a broader audience. 
* Name-calling, personal attacks, inflammatory remarks, or attempts to provoke a reaction will result in immediate removal.

### 2. **DON'T SHOOT THE MESSENGER**
* It is important to remember that the Blizzard employees who respond or provide context on GitHub may not be, and often are not, responsible for the decisions they are explaining. Singling them out for relaying that information is not productive.
  * Your criticism may be directed at someone who already agrees with you, and responding this way only discourages individual contributors from continuing to communicate and provide context on Blizzard’s behalf.
